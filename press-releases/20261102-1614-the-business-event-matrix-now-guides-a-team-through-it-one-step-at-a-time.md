# The Business Event Matrix Now Guides a Team Through It One Step at a Time

**2 November 2026**

AgileDataGuides today released an 8 Steps tab for the Business Event Matrix app, so a team can build a matrix one question at a time instead of facing an empty grid.

## The Problem

The Business Event Matrix showed everything on one grid: Core Business Events as rows, Domains and Concepts as columns, a ✓ or a ✭ in each cell. That grid is the right view once the matrix is full. For a team starting from a blank matrix, it gave no way in. People did not know whether to start with the Events or the Concepts, so they added a few of each and then clicked cells at random. Events were left with no Domain, Concepts were added that no Event used, and the driving Concept, the ✭ that says which Concept an Event creates, was often forgotten. A Facilitator running a session had to hold the order in their head. The Concept Model app already walked a team through its method one step at a time, and the Business Event Matrix had nothing like it.

## The Solution

The app now has an **8 Steps** tab, the first tab, before the **Matrix** tab, and the app opens on it. Eight steps run down the left, each with a line that reads off the matrix, for example "3 Domains" or "5 of 7 Events placed". Click a step and its panel opens on the right, with the step's question, one line on what to capture, and the place to capture it. A Back and Next bar under the panel names the step before and the step after. The Matrix tab is unchanged and still shows the whole matrix on one grid.

## How It Works

The steps follow the matrix: name the matrix and its Scope, identify the Domains, identify the Core Business Events, place each Event in its Domains, identify the Concepts by the 7W's, link the Concepts to each Event, mark each Event's driving Concept, and check the matrix for gaps. Add fields keep the cursor after Enter, so a Facilitator can type as fast as people talk. The marking steps show one card per Event with a chip for each Domain or Concept, and a click on a chip writes the same ✓ or ✭ the matrix cell holds. The last step lists what is missing, such as an Event with no driving Concept or a Concept no Event uses, and goes straight to the step that fixes it. The steps are a checklist, never a wizard: every step is one click away at any time, and no step locks another. The 8 Steps tab also shows inside the Context Plane, beside the matrix.

## Key Benefits

- **A way in for a blank matrix**: the team answers one question at a time, in an order that builds on the step before
- **Built for the room**: Enter adds and keeps the cursor, and one click marks a cell
- **Gaps found before they cost anything**: the last step names every Event without a Domain or a driving Concept, and every unused Domain or Concept
- **Nothing to relearn**: the same marks, the same edit popup, and the full matrix one tab away

The 8 Steps tab is available now in the Business Event Matrix app in the Context Plane monorepo, and it reaches the public app at [github.com/AgileDataGuides/business-event-matrix](https://github.com/AgileDataGuides/business-event-matrix) and its [live demo](https://agiledataguides.github.io/business-event-matrix) on its next publish.
