# The Business Event Matrix Now Opens on the SaaS Revenue Example

**6 November 2026**

AgileDataGuides today changed the Business Event Matrix app so that a first visit opens on the SaaS Revenue Events Matrix, the same SaaS story the Information Product Canvas, the Data Contract and the Access app tell.

## The Problem

The Business Event Matrix ships three example matrices: the SaaS Revenue Events Matrix, Lawrence Corr's BEAM example, and a small Ice Cream Shop. When nobody had opened a matrix before, the app opened the first one in its list, and the Ice Cream Shop came first. So a first-time visitor to the live demo, or someone who had just installed the app, landed on a four-Event teaching example about ice cream. The Information Product Canvas, the Data Contract and the Access app all use the SaaS revenue story as their main example, with its Customers, Subscriptions and Invoices. A person moving from one of those canvases to the Business Event Matrix lost the thread, and had to find the SaaS matrix in the switcher themselves.

## The Solution

The app now opens on the SaaS Revenue Events Matrix when no matrix was open before. A person who chooses another matrix still gets that matrix back the next time they open the app. The Ice Cream Shop and the BEAM example stay one click away in the matrix switcher.

## Key Benefits

- **One story across the canvases**: the matrix a visitor sees first matches the SaaS example in the Information Product Canvas, the Data Contract and the Access app
- **A full example first**: seven Core Business Events from ad click to cancellation, four Domains and 24 Concepts, not a four-Event sketch
- **Your choice still wins**: the matrix you last opened is the one that opens next time

The change is available now in the Business Event Matrix app in the Context Plane monorepo, the public app at [github.com/AgileDataGuides/business-event-matrix](https://github.com/AgileDataGuides/business-event-matrix), and its [live demo](https://agiledataguides.github.io/business-event-matrix).
